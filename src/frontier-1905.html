    What made the cut

    An idea links straight to 1905 if the paper uses it, Einstein later named it, or historians make a solid case for it. Dashed lines are the ones still under deliberation.

    From there, each idea is traced back through the ones that shaped it.

    The map leans European. Partly that's what Einstein read, and partly it's whose work got written down and kept. Voigt and Larmor wrote similar equations before 1905 but aren't here, as there's no sign Einstein knew their work.

    Light quanta, built on Planck's constant, is the one idea here that did not feed relativity. It is Einstein's other step of 1905, made three months earlier. The two support each other: with the aether gone, light no longer needs a medium to ripple through, so it can travel as packets of its own. Planck, in turn, was the first to champion relativity.

    The 1905 paper cites no sources and thanks only Michele Besso. Links follow Einstein's later accounts and the work of Gerald Holton, Abraham Pais, John Stachel and Peter Galison.

    The rim of the circle is 1905. Ideas beyond it came after and are not placed to scale.
